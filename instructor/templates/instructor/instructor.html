Why teach here

Built so teaching doesn't eat your calendar.

You bring the expertise. We handle the logistics, the classroom, and the students who actually show up ready to build.

01

Small, committed cohorts

Groups of 8–15 students who applied to be there, not a lecture hall of 200 you'll never get to know.

02

We handle the admin

Scheduling, room bookings, and student comms are on us. You focus on the syllabus and the sessions.

03

Paid, not volunteer

Every instructor is compensated per cohort — this is a real engagement, not a favor you're doing us.

What we look for

Practitioners over credentials.

Experience

Shipped work

You've built and shipped something real — a product, a codebase, a design system, a dataset in production.

Craft

Any of our tracks

Web development, product design, AI & data science, IoT, or robotics — depth in one beats breadth across five.

Teaching

Can explain, not just do

No teaching certificate required. What matters is being able to break down how you think, out loud, patiently.

Time

Room in your week

Most instructors run one 2-hour session a week plus async Q&A, for the length of a single cohort.
